# VB Local Guide: text message terms and privacy

**VB Local Guide** is a free, volunteer-run community demo that answers questions about Virginia Beach and Hampton Roads by text message. It is **not** an official City of Virginia Beach service.

## How it works
- You start the conversation by texting a question to our number. That's how you opt in.
- We send one automated reply for each message you send. We never send marketing or messages you didn't ask for.
- Message frequency depends on how many questions you send. Msg & data rates may apply.
- Reply **HELP** for help. Reply **STOP** to opt out at any time, and you won't get any more messages. Reply **START** to opt back in.

## Accuracy
Answers are generated by AI (xAI's Grok) from public information and may be wrong or out of date. Always check with the official source, such as virginiabeach.gov or VB311 at 757-385-3111. For emergencies, call 911.

## Privacy
- We use your message text only to generate a reply. It's sent to our providers: Twilio, which delivers texts, xAI, which writes answers, and Cloudflare, which hosts the service.
- We don't sell, rent or share phone numbers or opt-in data with third parties for marketing.
- Our providers may keep message logs for a limited time under their own policies.
- Please don't text sensitive personal information.
